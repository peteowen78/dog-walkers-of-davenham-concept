The pack rules

  • Four dogs. Never five.We will never walk more than four dogs at any time.
  • No muddy paws indoors.After every walk your dog is towel dried and offered plenty of fresh water.
  • Off the lead when we’re sure.We get to know your dog on their first walks. Once we trust their recall and you have signed the Off Lead form, off they go.
  • Away? No charge.Unlike most dog walking companies, we do not charge you to reserve your dog’s space. Just give us as much notice as you can.

Who’s holding the lead?

Ann

Lives in Hartford and has always been surrounded by animals: hamsters, guinea pigs, rabbits, water dragons, a chameleon, and Timmy, a 20 year old leopard tortoise.

Her sprocker spaniel Skye is a qualified search and rescue dog. Ann is a qualified canine first aider.

Andy

Ann’s son. He grew up with dogs around him and has been walking with Ann since July 2020.

He has a diploma in Animal Welfare from Reaseheath College and previously worked with birds of prey.

From hello to first walk

  1. You get in touch.
  2. We confirm the cost and when we can come.
  3. We email you the booking forms, with our insurance and check certificates if you’d like them.
  4. We come round to meet you and your dog, and pick up any keys.
  5. Walkies.

We accept cash and online transfer, and email an invoice at the end of each calendar month.
