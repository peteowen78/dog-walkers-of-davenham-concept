What we always do

  • Walk four dogs at mostWe will never walk more than four dogs at any time.
  • Dry them offAfter all our dog walks your dog will be towel dried to prevent any muddy paws in the house.
  • Keep leads on until we’re sureWe get to know your dog first. Off the lead only once we trust their recall and you have signed the Off Lead form.
  • Travel them properlyOur dog van has custom made dog cages and air conditioning, for a comfortable and stress free journey.
  • Hold your place for freeUnlike most dog walking companies, we do not charge you to reserve your dog’s space while they’re away.
  • Squeeze in the cuddlesOn home visits we feed, change water, give any medication, and give as many cuddles as we can squeeze into a visit.

Getting started

  1. You get in touch.
  2. We confirm the cost and our availability.
  3. We send a confirmation email with booking forms and, if you’d like them, our criminal record check and pet business insurance certificates.
  4. We come and meet you and your pet, and you hand over any keys.
  5. Services can begin.

We are happy to walk any breed of dog. Each one is assessed at that first meeting.
