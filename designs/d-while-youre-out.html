What the hour looks like

A ride in the van
Our dog van has been professionally fitted with custom made dog cages and air conditioning.
Four dogs at most
We will never walk more than four dogs at any time.
Off the lead, once we’re sure
We will only let your dog off the lead if we feel we can trust their recall, and once you have signed an Off Lead Disclaimer Form.
Home dry, with a drink
After all our dog walks your dog will be towel dried to prevent any muddy paws in the house and offered plenty of fresh water.

All our dog walks are conducted in accordance with the NarpsUK Responsible Dog Walking terms and conditions.

Meanwhile, on the walk

We love nothing more than to see dogs having fun and exercising through play and exploration.

Who has the lead

Ann lives in Hartford and has always been surrounded by animals. She is a qualified canine first aider.

Her sprocker spaniel Skye is a qualified scent specific search and rescue dog. Abby, her labradoodle, is training for her Kennel Club awards.

Andy is Ann’s son, and has been walking with her since July 2020.

He has a diploma in Animal Welfare from Reaseheath College and previously worked with birds of prey.

Registered with NarpsUK. Fully insured. Criminal record checked.
